How many matches does the starter kit cover?+

Ten complete match days when you use PREP, RALLY, and RESET each time. You can use each stage independently.

Does PREP contain caffeine?+

No. The current PREP concept is intentionally caffeine-free for evening racquet sport.

Can I change the refill mix?+

Yes. The subscription is designed to let players skip, pause, swap products, or change frequency before billing.

Are the formulas final?+

No. This launch prototype shows the intended product architecture. Final ingredients, dosage, testing, and approved claims require formulation and regulatory review.
