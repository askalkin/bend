# BEND Layout Direction and Media Backlog

## Layout Rule

Each section has one primary customer question and one dominant content type. Consecutive sections must not repeat a full-width media surface, card grid, split-screen composition, or large glass container.

Glass is a focus treatment, not a page background. Use no more than one dominant glass surface in a viewport. The continuous website canvas is BEND Light Grey `#EEF0EC`.

## Section Purposes

| Section | Customer question | Primary purpose | Dominant content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header | Where am I and where can I go? | Orientation | Wordmark, three links, bag |
| Hero | What is BEND? | Explain the offer in five seconds | Open headline and one colorful offer card |
| Routine | How does the system work? | Make PREP, RALLY, RESET memorable | One connected three-step list |
| First Box | What arrives? | Establish contents and value | Open inventory typography and one accent card |
| Timing | Why is it organized this way? | Connect the routine to match rhythm | One court-timing instrument and supporting text |
| Formula | Can I understand what I am taking? | Build product trust | Disclosure list inside one focused glass card |
| Protocol | What do I actually do? | Remove usage uncertainty | Open linear timeline |
| Shop | What am I buying? | Enable one purchase decision | Open product copy and one order card |
| FAQ | What could stop me buying? | Resolve objections | Text-only accordion |
| Footer | How do I stay connected? | Capture interest and leave brand memory | Compact form and expanding BEND wordmark |

## Rhythm Guardrails

- Alternate open, structured, open, focused-card, open, transactional compositions.
- Do not place two viewport-height media or card surfaces next to each other in the scroll.
- Use one headline, one supporting paragraph, and one action per section.
- Use Druk Bold for identity and display typography, with Druk Bold Italic reserved for movement words and selected data. Use Work Sans for communication and interface copy.
- The current Druk files are labeled demo/trial. Replace them with properly licensed production webfonts before commercial launch.
- Use one card radius: `32px`. Fully round only controls and dose forms.
- Keep gradients inside a focal card or small atmospheric field.
- Express court energy with rules, grids, trajectory arcs, score notation, and moving acid points.
- Reserve glass for the hero offer, formula disclosure, and order surface, separated by open sections.

## Packaging Assets

### P0: Industrial Design

- Stainless-steel court bottle: final dimensions, volume, grip, closure, cap, coating, finish, and cleaning method.
- Bottle graphics: front, back, side, base, material markings, wash instructions, and print or engraving method.
- PREP refill tube: structure, cap, label system, dose count, storage, and tamper evidence.
- RALLY refill tube: structure, cap, label system, dose count, storage, and tamper evidence.
- RESET refill tube: structure, cap, label system, dose count, storage, and tamper evidence.
- Individual PREP dose: approved white ball shape, size, texture, weight, and wrapper.
- Individual RALLY dose: approved acid tennis-ball shape, seam detail, size, texture, weight, and wrapper.
- Individual RESET dose: approved translucent fruit gummy shape, size, texture, weight, and wrapper.

### P0: Starter Box

- Structural dieline for every panel.
- Internal insert holding the bottle, three tubes, and doses.
- Opening sequence and contents map.
- Shipping protection and drop-test requirements.
- Print finishes: white substrate, chrome detail, emboss or deboss, spot gloss, and color proof.
- Front, back, side, top, bottom, and inside-panel artwork.
- Physical prototype and production proof.

### P0: Regulatory System

- Ingredient list and nutrition data.
- Serving amounts and directions for PREP, RALLY, and RESET.
- Allergen, warning, contraindication, and storage copy.
- Net weight, batch, expiry, operator, origin, barcode, and recycling marks.
- Market-specific language versions and claim review.

### P1: Refill and Printed Matter

- Thirty-dose refill carton or mailer.
- Refill protective insert and tamper seal.
- First-use guide.
- Three-step instruction card.
- Formula and ingredient sheet.
- Refill-management and cancellation insert.
- Production masters: CAD files, dielines, Pantone and CMYK references, finish samples, and printer-ready artwork.
