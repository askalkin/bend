Concept Scope

BEND Packaging System

A complete packaging direction for the BEND court nutrition ritual, showing the starter kit, refill architecture, reusable vessels, and individual product expressions.

  1. Premium starter box The first brand encounter: protective, giftable, and designed to frame the system as a ritual.
  2. Molded mycelium insert A tactile compostable tray that gives each object a precise place while keeping the unboxing elevated.
  3. Reusable court vessel A signature bottle or cup adapted per concept, extending the identity beyond the supplement pack.
  4. PREP container The before-play format, built around a compact daily dose and a calm preparation moment.
  5. RALLY container The during-play format, designed for easy court-bag use and a clear mid-session behavior.
  6. RESET container The after-play format, with the most sensory product expression through a larger glossy gummy.
  7. Portable match tubes Slim refillable carry pieces for one match, one court bag, and one focused use occasion.
  8. Refill architecture Lightweight replenishment packs that make repeat purchase feel intentional, premium, and low-waste.
  9. Supplement formats A recognizable green product language across small PREP tablets, medium RALLY tablets, and RESET gummies.
  10. Printed ritual card A small supporting touchpoint for dosage, replenishment, brand storytelling, and digital content.

Visual Discovery

Recognition Beyond Color

The system can make PREP, RALLY, and RESET instantly recognizable through shape, material, cap construction, tab placement, and micro-marking. Color stays as a precise secondary cue: useful for quick scanning, but never the whole identity.
